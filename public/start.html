Use it inside Claude

The AI runs on your own Claude account. A free account works.

  1. Create a free Claude account. Go to claude.ai and sign up with your email or Google account.
  2. Open Consult Scribe with the button below, on the same phone or computer. Sign in if asked.
  3. Pick the output you want, such as Clinical notes or Carer plan.
  4. Talk using your keyboard's mic key. Tap the big mic, then the mic on your keyboard (Gboard or Samsung on Android, the iPhone keyboard, or Windows + H on a PC). Tap Done when finished.
  5. Tap Create, then check the result before using it.
